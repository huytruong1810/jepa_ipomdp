Research progress · 30 September 2026

A learned belief filter that plans as well as the exact Bayes solution

Method

How the agent acts, plans and learns, and what checks it

Representation choices

A single latent vector, no object slots and no observation decoder. The two-hot codec decodes linearly in real space, so the −100/+10 door reward averages to −45 as it should.

Exact references

An exact batched Bayes filter and alpha-vector value iteration by incremental pruning, with a proven LP-computed error bound. Every statistic on this page compares against these.

Prediction scores against the observations that occurred

A perfect model cannot beat the exact model's scores, because growls are 85% accurate and door observations are uniform noise. The learned model matches them, and its KL to the exact prediction is 10⁻⁴ nats. Means over 5 seeds.

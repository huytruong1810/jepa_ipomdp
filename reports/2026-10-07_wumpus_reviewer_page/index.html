Wumpus World: the learned belief agent now rarely dies, but earns a third of what exact beliefs allow

Domain

Single-hunter 4×4 Wumpus World, 50-step games, no exact solver

Rules

Hidden state2 pits, 1 wumpus and 1 gold on distinct non-start cells: 16,380 equally likely layouts, plus the hunter's cell, heading, arrow and whether the wumpus lives.
ActionsForward, turn left, turn right, grab, shoot. Grabbing the gold wins. There is no climb action.
PerceptsStench, breeze, glitter, bump and scream bits: 32 observations, plus one for game over. The start cell's percepts arrive before the first action.
Rewards−1 per action, −10 for a shot, +1000 for the gold, −1000 for death. γ = 0.99. A game ends at the first win or death, or after 50 steps.
Ground truthAn exact filter enumerates all 16,380 layouts per game. It gives the true belief to score the latent against, but no optimal policy.
AgentThe same belief-tree planner and training loop as on Tiger. Here the belief filter learns by predicting the next observation (the decoder variant). JEPA learned the danger ahead less well in offline tests.
BoundsKnowing the layout is worth 936 on average, which is an upper bound. A hand-written agent acting on the exact belief earns 573, which is a lower bound on what a belief-based agent can reach.
